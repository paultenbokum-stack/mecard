# Smart business card industry: running notes

Append to this file each week. Facts are tagged by confidence. Most sources are vendor blogs, so treat them as indicative.

## Market size (first noted 2026-11-09)
- Global digital business card market: about $217M to $243M in 2026, growth about 9% to 12% a year, about $680M by 2035 on the most bullish estimate. Estimates conflict. (Sources: [Wave Connect roundup](https://wavecnct.com/digital-business-card-statistics), [GII Research](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/sky1914042-digital-business-card-market-size-share-growth.html))
- NFC segment: about $150M in 2024, projected $450M by 2033. (Unverified.)
- IT and software is the largest buyer segment (about 32% in 2024).
- Adoption is low in some markets: under 5% of Australian professionals use digital or NFC cards as a primary networking tool (early 2026). (Source: [Paperlust](https://printshop.paperlust.co/blog?p=1431))
- Buyer concern: phishing and reluctance to share digital cards.

## Competitors and pricing (from comparison articles, many by rivals)
- HiHello: Business plan about $5 per user per month on annual billing.
- Blinq: published pricing, minimum 5 seats on Business (about $24.95 a month).
- Popl: team pricing is by quote; one figure cites a median around $17,500 a year (unverified).
- Linq: free, premium and teams plans, no team price found.
- Positioning gap for MeCard: a once-off Pro price (R199) with no subscription is unusual against the per-seat monthly norm. Worth leaning on.
- Sources: [Blinq cost comparison](https://blinq.me/blog/comparing-costs-of-digital-business-card-platforms-in-2025-blinq), [Kado Networks Linq vs Popl](https://www.kadonetworks.com/blog/linq-vs-popl---choose-the-best-one)

## MeCard-specific learnings
- Revenue is lumpy and team-led (bulk card orders by bank transfer or PayFast). Individual Pro upgrades are R199.
- Event tracking began 26 Oct 2026. Contact taps and saves definitions changed on 7 Nov 2026.
- Attributable acquisition is thin: Google organic to `/cards/`, plus chatgpt.com referrals.
