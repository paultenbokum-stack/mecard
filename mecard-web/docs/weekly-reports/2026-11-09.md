## Suggestions for the week ahead (max 3)
1. **Test the Pro upsell on the profile row.** It carries about 80% of upsell impressions at about 1% CTR, and nothing was clicked this week. Change the copy to lead with "once off R199, no yearly fee" (the 8 Nov message) and add one social-proof line. Success: 5+ clicks and 2+ checkouts from that placement.
2. **Handle teams intent differently.** Teams signups go nowhere (0 of 3 published) while team orders are all of this week's revenue. Route teams-intent signups to a short "tell us your team size" step or a human follow-up, link `/for-teams/` from the home page, and give the new salesperson referral links to a few resellers.
3. **Capture the search and AI-referral trickle.** Google and chatgpt.com are the only attributable sources. Publish a plain-language "NFC business card South Africa: price, how it works, vs paper" page with a free-profile call to action, and add UTM tags to anything posted so direct traffic (80% of signups) can be split.

Samples are tiny (4 to 6 signups a week), so week on week percentages swing wildly. `accounts.last_active_at` reads zero active in 7 days, which looks like an unpopulated field, not a true zero. Only 19 profiles have any views in the last 30 days.
