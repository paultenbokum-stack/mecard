- 25 Oct: cutover to new Cloud Run project (mecard-512711), conversion tracking + insights MCP, reCAPTCHA dropped, sign-up "Just me / For my team", saved addresses at checkout, Pro upgrade before payment. Effect: views dipped to 4 on cutover day then recovered to 50–78/day; tracking now live.

- **Data-quality bug:** `upsell_viewed` = 466 events from 14 users; one user alone produced 240 in 2.5 hours, and placement `manage_profile_row` accounts for 361. Impressions are firing per render, not once per view. Upsell exposure numbers are unusable until deduped.
- Pro-upsell clicks: 4 from 3 users vs 466 impressions.

## Suggestions for the week ahead (max 3)
1. **Dedupe upsell_viewed** (once per user/placement/offer per session or per N minutes) so the upsell funnel is measurable. Prerequisite for every conversion experiment in docs/conversion-plan.md.
2. **Turn the recipient moment into acquisition.** 68% of views come from NFC taps and ~26% of phone viewers save the card, but viral CTA click-through is 0 of 40. Test a single clear line on the card page ("Get your own free MeCard") shown after a save/tap, and track it as a `split` experiment.
3. **Follow up the new signups.** Of 6 sign-ups, 4 published and 0 got a first external view; the two ChatGPT-referred users landed on /free/. Add a "share your card / tap-to-test" step after onboarding preview and an email nudge when no external view in 48 h. Also chase the R1,904 on-hold team order (bacs) and the processing R398 payfast order.
