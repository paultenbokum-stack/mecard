## Market (2 Nov 2026; vendor/research-listing figures, low reliability)
- Digital business card market estimated ~USD 217–238m in 2026, CAGR 9–12% (different publishers disagree). NFC segment ~USD 150m (2024) → ~450m (2033) in one forecast.
- One survey summary: 37% of businesses use digital cards (16% in 2020); tech sector 72%.
- Barrier cited: reluctance to share/tap due to phishing concerns, so trust cues matter.
